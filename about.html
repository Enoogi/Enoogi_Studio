About Enoogi.Studio

Hello, I'm Inwook.

I paint the ones we love — pets, people, the small moments worth keeping — one at a time, in watercolour and ink.

The hand behind the work

One at a time,
never replicated.

My work is rooted in simplicity and intention. Watercolour lets me build depth, softness and warmth, while ink keeps the line clean and the form alive. Whether you choose a full watercolour portrait or a minimal sketch, every piece is made by hand and never reproduced.

I believe meaningful art should feel personal. I work closely from your reference photos and approach every commission with care, patience and respect for the subject — pieces made to celebrate loved ones, honour pets, and hold on to special moments.

Each artwork is original, hand-painted, and made to be cherished for years to come.

Inwook Kim · Founder & artist

No two are ever the same, and none are reproduced. I hope yours feels like it was made only for you — because it was.

Materials & craft

Made to last a lifetime.

01 — Paper

Cotton, archival

Heavyweight 100% cotton watercolour paper — acid-free and archival, so colour stays true for decades.

02 — Pigment & ink

Layered by hand

Artist-grade watercolour and ink, built up slowly in washes. Lightfast and luminous — painted, never printed.

03 — Framing

Finished in oak

Each original is set in a solid oak frame and packed with care — ready to hang the day it arrives.
The studio

A small, quiet room.

Enoogi.Studio is a home studio where every portrait is painted by hand, one at a time. When a piece is finished, it's signed, carefully packed, and shipped worldwide — flat and tracked, so it arrives exactly as it left the desk.
